# BaoBao privacy policy (draft)

**Status: draft.** This describes what the app does today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be before it is published or linked from a store listing.

Last updated: 2026-11-11.

## The short version

BaoBao keeps what you tell it on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

## What BaoBao stores

| Data | Why | Where |
|------|-----|-------|
| The name you give your profile | To greet you | On your device, in an encrypted database |
| Your mood check-ins and when you made them | To choose BaoBao's replies and to notice when you have been low for a while | On your device, in an encrypted database |
| Where your last conversation got to | To remember it | On your device, in an encrypted database |
| Stars, items you own, what you have equipped, claw-machine tries and statistics | To run the game and shop | On your device, in an encrypted database |
| Volume, accessibility and app-lock settings, and the region you chose for helplines | To remember your preferences | On your device, in app settings |

The database is encrypted with a key held in your device's secure key storage (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain). The key never leaves the device.

## What BaoBao does not do

- It does not create an account or ask for an email address, phone number, or password.
- It does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- It does not read your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.
- It does not back your data up to the cloud. On Android, backups are switched off for this app.

## Biometrics

If you switch on the app lock, your device checks your fingerprint, face, or PIN. BaoBao only learns whether the check passed. It never sees or stores your biometric data.

## Links to helplines

The "Get help" screen can start a phone call, a text message, or open a website. When you tap one of these, your phone or browser handles it, and the service you contact has its own privacy practices.

## Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete my data. This permanently removes that profile's check-ins, conversations, stars, and items. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

## Children

BaoBao is intended for people aged 13 and over.

## Not medical care

BaoBao is a companion for everyday feelings. It is not therapy, diagnosis, or a crisis service.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, the app will ask you to agree again before continuing.
